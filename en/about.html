Sourcing as it stands

What relying on trade fairs looks like

When fairs are the only route to new products, three things follow.

01

The date you get to look is fixed for you

Fairs happen once or twice a year for three days. If you need something new in between, you wait for the next one or reorder from your usual supplier.

02

Every trip costs again

Flights, hotels, an interpreter, and days away from work. The next fair costs the same again.

03

One quote takes weeks

On site you trade cards and brochures. Back home you email and wait, and the moment you needed it passes.

How pricing works

Prices are sent only to verified buyers

Unit price, minimum order quantity, lead time and supply terms are sent only to verified accounts. They are not masked on screen; before verification the data is not sent at all.

Frequently asked

Before you sign up

Does it cost anything?
Signing up, verifying, browsing and requesting quotes are all free. No membership fee and no commission.
Why does seeing prices require verification?
It is the condition on which manufacturers publish real prices. If anyone could see them, they would not post them.
Can I order a small quantity first?
It depends on the product. Those tagged negotiable can discuss smaller orders, so state your target quantity when you enquire.
Do I pay through MAKENOV?
No. Requests go straight to the manufacturer, and you contract and pay with them. We add no margin.
Can I get documents for import registration?
Select the documents you need on the quote form: ingredient list, certificate of origin, test report and catalogue.
Which countries are the products from?
Manufacturers worldwide can list. Origin is shown on each product, and the catalogue keeps growing.
